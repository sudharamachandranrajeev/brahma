# Changelog

## 0.2.1

- Phones get new navigation: a floating dock that scrolls through every screen (the "More"
  sheet is gone) and a side panel listing them all with what each is for. Swipe right on any
  page, or tap the dock's menu button, to open it; Back closes it.

## 0.2.0

A new look, after a dark instrument-panel reference: a charcoal canvas with faint contour lines,
hairline cards, one lime accent for the best or chosen, big tabular figures under small spaced
capitals, and a floating navigation bar. Light mode keeps the layout with ink actions. Inter is
bundled.

- Garage leads with the next date due, or the cheapest car in play, and counts.
- Finance leads with the lowest real cost a month; plans are ranked rows that open for details.
- The car page shows its dates as tiles, and the logbook totals as figures.

## 0.1.0

First release: the car side of Anveshana (0.2.0) as its own app. Homes are now in Graha Anveshana.

- Search links for the main UK and Northern Ireland car sites, and your own.
- Garage with stages, price check against similar saved cars, red flags and price history.
- MOT, tax, insurance and service dates, logbook, PCP mileage pace, dealer-rights dates.
- Buying, collection-day and selling checklists; the cost of owning a car once sold.
- Cash, loan, HP, PCP and lease compared by real cost; running costs; a buyer's guide.
- Encrypted on the device; optional end-to-end encrypted sync on the shared Brahma account.

Cars saved in Anveshana 0.2.0 are not carried over automatically: add them again from their
advert links, or turn on sync.
