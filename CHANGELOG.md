# Changelog

## 0.1.0

First release: the car side of Anveshana (0.2.0) as its own app. Homes are now in Graha Anveshana.

- Search links for the main UK and Northern Ireland car sites, and your own.
- Garage with stages, price check against similar saved cars, red flags and price history.
- MOT, tax, insurance and service dates, logbook, PCP mileage pace, dealer-rights dates.
- Buying, collection-day and selling checklists; the cost of owning a car once sold.
- Cash, loan, HP, PCP and lease compared by real cost; running costs; a buyer's guide.
- Encrypted on the device; optional end-to-end encrypted sync on the shared Brahma account.

Cars saved in Anveshana 0.2.0 are not carried over automatically: add them again from their
advert links, or turn on sync.
